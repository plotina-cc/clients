# Live proof run — 2026-11-10

Record of what `stream_video_toggle_proof.mjs` measured, kept next to the harness.
Element Call v0.26.1 + patches 0001–0005, built by `build.py --mode source` on this
branch — the artefact it produced is `plotina-element-call.tar.gz`
sha256 `f2214baccc7c18597c10712bd084155309421e9d06fbdafc74a13bbe612bd490`, 171
files — and served over HTTPS with `serve_bundle.mjs`. One local stand (Synapse,
`lk-jwt-service`, LiveKit SFU, the upstream `docker-compose-dev.yml` nginx).
Three browser contexts in one call.

Result: all twelve checks passed (`EXIT=0`), on three separate runs of the same
harness: twice against a plain `vite build` of the patched tree, and once against
the `build.py --mode source` artefact above.

The share is identified by difference rather than by pixel size: LiveKit chooses
the simulcast layer per subscriber, so the same share arrived as 640x360 for the
viewer here and as 320x180 for the control viewer in one run. Both runs below show
the viewer receiving exactly one more video stream before hiding than after.

Same moment, control viewer (never touched the toggle): share still there — 640x360
@ 10 fps, 25 frames, 76.0 KB. The sharer is untouched too (its two inbound camera
streams keep flowing at 50 frames).

The share returns on a new inbound SSRC, i.e. a real re-subscription, not a
re-render.
